Shared bibliography

Advanced notes: contents

All chapters use the same author–year citation style and bibliography. Their reference sections list the works cited in each chapter; the complete list appears below. Further-reading suggestions explain the role of selected sources rather than implying that every source supports every model extension.

Reading by topic

Topic Starting points
Selection-index foundations Hazel (1943)
Mixed-model prediction Henderson (1975); Robinson (1991)
Pedigree precision and genomic relationships Henderson (1976); VanRaden (2008)
Estimation, constraints and uncertainty PATTERSON and THOMPSON (1971); Gilmour et al. (1995); Self and Liang (1987); Kackar and Harville (1984)
Identifiability and maternal components Rothenberg (1971); Willham (1972)
Longitudinal covariance and G×E Meyer (1998); Meyer (2001); Su et al. (2006)
Factor-analytic models Thompson et al. (2003)
Bayesian inference and marker mixtures Sorensen and Gianola (2002); Habier et al. (2011); Erbe et al. (2012); Moser et al. (2015)
Biological-class priors and molecular information MacLeod et al. (2016); Liu et al. (2022)
Sparse and iterative computation Chen et al. (2008); Saad (2003); Higham (2002)
Stochastic traces Hutchinson (1989); Avron and Toledo (2011)
Validation and calibration Daetwyler et al. (2013); Runcie and Cheng (2019); Legarra and Reverter (2018)
Recent estimation and prediction studies Bermann et al. (2025); Yang et al. (2025); Gowane et al. (2026)
